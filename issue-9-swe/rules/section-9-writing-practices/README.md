# Section 9 - Writing practices

Source: [Issue 9, Section 9 - Writing practices](../../../issue-9/part-1-writing-rules/section-9-writing-practices/README.md) gives the full text and the original examples.

## In software text

Section 9 is about the method of writing, not about one word or one punctuation mark. Do not replace each word that is not approved with the first alternative in the dictionary. Think about the meaning of the sentence, then write a sentence that gives the same meaning ([Rule 9.1](rule-9-01.md)). Use the same terms in all the text of a repository ([Rule 9.4](rule-9-04.md)). The general recommendations ([GR-1 to GR-8](general-recommendations/README.md)) are not rules, but they prevent frequent errors.
