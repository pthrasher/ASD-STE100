# X, Y, Z

<!-- page 433 | 2-1-Y1 -->
## YES (adv)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: Function word that shows the positive answer to a question
  - STE: DOES THE LIGHT COME ON? YES OR NO?

## yet (conj)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: BUT (conj)
  - STE: TIGHTEN THE NUTS, BUT KEEP THEM SUFFICIENTLY LOOSE TO REMOVE THEM WITH YOUR HAND.
  - Non-STE: The nuts should be tightened, yet loose enough to remove by hand.

## yet (adv)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: AT THIS TIME
  - STE: DO NOT REMOVE THE FIXTURE COVER AT THIS TIME.
  - Non-STE: Do not remove the fixture cover yet.

## YOU (pron)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: The reader or the user
  - STE: YOU CAN CONTINUE THE TEST.
  - STE: HOT SURFACES CAN BURN YOU.

## YOUR (adj)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: Related to the reader or the user
  - STE: IF YOU GET SOLVENT IN YOUR EYES, FLUSH THEM IMMEDIATELY WITH WATER.

## yourself (pron)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: YOU (pron)
  - STE: DO NOT USE YOUR HANDS TO PREVENT MOVEMENT OF THE LP COMPRESSOR. IF YOU DO, THE LP COMPRESSOR CAN CAUSE YOU INJURY AND CAN CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE EQUIPMENT.
  - Non-STE: Do not use your hands to prevent movement of the LP compressor. If you do, you can cause injury to yourself and damage to the equipment.

## zero (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: ZERO (TN)
  - STE: ADJUST THE METER TO ZERO.
  - Non-STE: Zero the meter.
