# Section 6 - Descriptive writing

## Summary of the rules

> **Content structure**
> - Rule [6.1](rule-6-01.md) Give information gradually.
> - Rule [6.2](rule-6-02.md) Use key words and key phrases to give your text a logical structure.
>
> **Sentences**
> - Rule [6.3](rule-6-03.md) Write short sentences. Use a maximum of 25 words in each sentence.
>
> **Paragraphs**
> - Rule [6.4](rule-6-04.md) Use paragraphs to show related information.
> - Rule [6.5](rule-6-05.md) Make sure that each paragraph has only one topic.
> - Rule [6.6](rule-6-06.md) Make sure that no paragraph has more than six sentences.

## Content structure

Descriptive writing gives information, not instructions. Thus, the imperative form of the verb is not permitted.

Descriptive writing can be:

- A description of an item, a product, a system, or a component, its function, how it is made and how it operates
- A text that gives general information (for example, reports, brochures, and papers)
- A note in a procedure.
