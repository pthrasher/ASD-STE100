# Rule 3.5

> **Rule 3.5** **Use the “-ing” form of a verb only as a technical noun or as a modifier in a technical noun.**

In English, words that have an “-ing” form can have different functions in a sentence (different parts of speech).

**General examples:**

Words that have an “-ing” form can be <u>part of a verb</u> to describe an action in the present.

- [Neutral] *Be careful while the door <u>is opening</u>.*

They can also be <u>adjectives</u>.

- [Neutral] *An <u>opening</u> door can be dangerous.*

They can be <u>nouns or parts of noun phrases</u>.

- [Neutral] *<u>Opening a door</u> can be dangerous.*

They can make <u>long groups of modifiers, noun phrases, and dependent clauses</u>.

- [Neutral] *<u>A mechanic opening a door without obeying the specified safety precautions</u> can easily cause injury to persons <u>standing near the door</u>.*

These different functions for words that have an “-ing” form can frequently cause ambiguity or long, complex sentences. Thus, words that have an “-ing” form are usually not permitted.

**Examples:**

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *When you are doing this procedure, obey all the safety precautions.*
- [STE] **STE:** When you do this procedure, obey all the safety precautions.

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *Mechanics wearing insufficient protective clothing and opening containers containing hazardous materials in areas where there is a lack of ventilation, using inappropriate tools without observing the manufacturer's instructions, are in danger of coming into contact with these materials and thus suffering from skin irritation and breathing problems.*
- [STE] **STE:** Before you use dangerous materials, obey these precautions:<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(1) Read the manufacturer's instructions.<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(2) Make sure that there is sufficient airflow in the work area.<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(3) Put on a face mask and protective clothing.<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(4) Get the correct tools to open the containers for these materials.<br>
  If you do not obey these precautions, injury to your skin and your lungs can occur.<br>

## Words that have an “-ing” form and are technical nouns or parts of technical nouns

You can use a word that has an “-ing” form as a technical noun (for example, in procedural titles or headings).

**Examples:**

- [STE] Cleaning, Testing and Fault Isolation, Handling, Packaging, Shipping, Troubleshooting

You can also use the “-ing” form of a verb as a modifier in a technical noun. This modifier is an adjective that is related to the function of a system, component, part, tool, material, or equipment.

**Examples:**

- [STE] Air-conditioning system, degreasing agent, grinding wheel, polishing disc, sanding machine, switching relay, welding torch

## Approved words that have an “-ing” form

Only a small number of approved words in the dictionary have an “-ing” form. They are:
- Nouns (lighting, opening, routing, and servicing)
- Adjectives (mating, missing, and remaining)
- A pronoun (something)
- A preposition (during).
