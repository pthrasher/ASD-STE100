# List of approved verbs

The table that follows gives you the list of the approved verbs in the dictionary. Use this list as a quick-reference tool to find the approved verbs.

**A** — ABSORB, ACCEPT, ACTIVATE, ADAPT, ADD, ADJUST, AGREE, ALIGN, APPLY, ARM, ASSEMBLE, ATTACH

**B** — BALANCE, BE, BECOME, BEND, BLEED, BLOW, BOND, BREAK, BREATHE, BURN, BYPASS

**C** — CALCULATE, CALIBRATE, CAN, CANCEL, CANNOT, CATCH, CAUSE, CHANGE, CHARGE, CLEAN, CLOSE, COLLECT, COME, COME ON, COMPARE, COMPLETE, COMPRESS, CONNECT, CONTACT, CONTAIN, CONTINUE, CONTROL, CORRECT, COUNT, CUT

**D** — DEACTIVATE, DECREASE, DE-ENERGIZE, DEFLATE, DEFUEL, DEPLOY, DISARM, DISASSEMBLE, DISCARD, DISCONNECT, DISENGAGE, DIVIDE, DO, DRAIN, DRINK, DRY

**E** — EAT, EJECT, ENERGIZE, ENGAGE, ERASE, EXAMINE, EXPAND, EXTEND, EXTINGUISH

**F** — FALL, FEATHER, FEEL, FILL, FIND, FIRE, FLASH, FLOW, FLUSH, FOLD, FOLLOW, FREEZE

**G** — GET, GIVE, GO, GO OFF, GROUND

**H** — HANG, HAVE, HEAR, HELP, HIT, HOLD

**I** — IDENTIFY, IGNORE, ILLUMINATE, INCLUDE, INCREASE, INFLATE, INSTALL, INTERCHANGE, ISOLATE

**J** — (none)

**K** — KEEP, KILL, KNOW

**L** — LATCH, LET, LIFT, LISTEN, LOCK, LOOK, LOOSEN, LOWER, LUBRICATE

**M** — MAKE, MAKE SURE, MEASURE, MELT, MIX, MONITOR, MOOR, MOVE, MULTIPLY, MUST

**N** — (none)

**O** — OBEY, OCCUR, OPEN, OPERATE, OVERRIDE

**P** — PAINT, PARK, POINT, POLISH, PREPARE, PRESSURIZE, PREVENT, PROTRUDE, PULL, PUSH, PUT, PUT ON

**Q** — (none)

**R** — READ, RECEIVE, RECOMMEND, RECORD, RECYCLE, REFER, REFUEL, REJECT, RELEASE, REMOVE, REPAIR, REPLACE, RETRACT, RUB

**S** — SAFETY, SCHEDULE, SEAL, SEE, SELECT, SEND, SENSE, SET, SHAKE, SHOW, SIMULATE, SMELL, SMOKE, SOAK, SPEAK, SPILL, SPRAY, START, STAY, STOP, STOW, SUBTRACT, SUPPLY, SWALLOW

**T** — TAG, TAP, TELL, THINK, TIGHTEN, TILT, TORQUE, TOUCH, TOW, TRANSMIT, TRY, TUNE, TURN, TWIST

**U** — UNFOLD, UNLOCK, UNWIND, USE

**V** — (none)

**W** — WAIT, WALK, WANT, WEAR, WEIGH, WILL, WIND, WRITE

**X, Y, Z** — (none)
