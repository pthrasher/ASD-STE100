# Q

<!-- page 347 | 2-1-Q1 -->
## qualified (adj)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: APPROVED (adj)
  - STE: AN APPROVED PERSON MUST DO THE IMPORTANT CHECKS.
  - Non-STE: A qualified person must do the important checks.

## QUALITY (n)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: Condition, property, or type
  - STE: THIS MEDICAL EQUIPMENT MUST BE OF GOOD QUALITY.

## QUANTITY (n)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: A specified amount or number
  - STE: APPLY A SMALL QUANTITY OF LUBRICATING OIL ON THE THREADS OF THE BOLTS.
- Help: For an amount that you can count, use NUMBER (n).
  - STE: MAKE SURE THAT YOU HAVE THE CORRECT NUMBER OF SHIMS AVAILABLE.

## quarterly (adv)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: INTERVAL (n)
  - STE: SEND REPORTS TO THE AUTHORITIES AT INTERVALS OF THREE MONTHS.
  - Non-STE: Send reports to the authorities quarterly.

## questionable (adj)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: NOT SURE
  - STE: IF YOU ARE NOT SURE OF THEIR CONDITION, REJECT THE PARTS.
  - Non-STE: Reject the parts that are in a questionable condition.

## quick (adj)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: QUICKLY (adv)
  - STE: MAKE SURE THAT THE ROD DOES NOT MOVE QUICKLY.
  - Non-STE: Make sure that there is no quick movement of the rod.

## QUICKLY (adv)
- Status: approved
- Meaning: In a fast manner
  - STE: MAKE SURE THAT THE PRESSURE DECREASES QUICKLY.

## quit (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: GO (v)
  - STE: BEFORE YOU START THE ENGINE, MAKE SURE THAT ALL PERSONS GO AWAY FROM THE DANGER AREA.
  - Non-STE: Before you start the engine, make sure that all persons quit the danger area.
- Alternative: STOP (v)
  - STE: IF THE RED LIGHT COMES ON, STOP THE REFUEL PROCEDURE.
  - Non-STE: If the red light comes on, quit the refuel procedure.

## quote (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: GIVE (v)
  - STE: THE TABLE GIVES THE MAXIMUM TOLERANCE FOR THE HOLE.
  - Non-STE: The table quotes the maximum tolerance for the hole.
