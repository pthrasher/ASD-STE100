# K

<!-- page 293 | 2-1-K1 -->
## KEEP (v)
- Status: approved
- Forms: KEEPS, KEPT, KEPT
- Meaning: To continue to have or hold
  - STE: KEEP THE COMPOUND IN A CLOSED CONTAINER.

## key (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: REFER (v)
  - STE: THE ILLUSTRATION REFERS TO EACH STEP.
  - Non-STE: The illustration will be keyed to each of the steps.
- Alternative: KEY (TN)
  - STE: INSTALL THE KEY BETWEEN THE PULLEY AND THE SHAFT.
  - Non-STE: The pulley must be keyed to the shaft.

## KILL (v)
- Status: approved
- Forms: KILLS, KILLED, KILLED
- Meaning: To cause death
  - STE: HIGH VOLTAGES CAN KILL YOU.

## kind (n)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: TYPE (n)
  - STE: YOU CAN USE TWO TYPES OF SEALING COMPOUND.
  - Non-STE: Two kinds of sealing compound can be used.

## kink (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: KINK (TN)
  - STE: DO NOT MAKE KINKS IN THE CABLES.
  - Non-STE: Do not kink the cables.

## knock (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: HIT (v)
  - STE: HIT THE CLAMP ON THE RIGHT SIDE WITH A HAMMER.
  - Non-STE: Knock the clamp on the right side with a hammer.

## KNOW (v)
- Status: approved
- Forms: KNOWS, KNEW, KNOWN
- Meaning: To be sure of data, to have data ready to use
  - STE: IF YOU KNOW THE CLEARANCE, YOU CAN CALCULATE THE THICKNESS OF THE WASHER.
