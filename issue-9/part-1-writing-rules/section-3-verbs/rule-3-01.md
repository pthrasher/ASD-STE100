# Rule 3.1

> **Rule 3.1** **Use only the verb forms that are given in the dictionary.**

The STE dictionary gives you the verb forms that you can use for each approved verb.

**Example:**

- [STE] **REMOVE (v)**<br>
  **REMOVES,**<br>
  **REMOVED,**<br>
  **REMOVED**<br>

- [STE] **GIVE (v)**<br>
  **GIVES,**<br>
  **GAVE,**<br>
  **GIVEN**<br>

<!-- page 68 | 1-3-2 -->
> **Help:** The introduction to the dictionary in part 2 gives you more information about the verb forms and how to use the approved verbs.
