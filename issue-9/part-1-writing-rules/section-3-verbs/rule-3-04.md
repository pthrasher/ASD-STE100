# Rule 3.4

> **Rule 3.4** **Do not use auxiliary verbs to make complex verb constructions.**

Do not use the past participle form as a verb form together with the auxiliary verb “have.” This construction will make a tense that is not approved.

**Example:**

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *The operator <u>has adjusted </u>the linkage.*

  (The present perfect tense is not approved.)
- [STE] **STE:** The operator <u>adjusted</u> the linkage.

  (The simple past tense is approved.)

Some complex verb constructions include other auxiliary verbs with the past participle form as a verb. Sentences with these constructions become complex sentences in the passive voice.

**Examples:**

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *The seat <u>is to be installed</u> before you install the cushion* <!-- sic -->
- [STE] **STE:** Before you install the cushion, <u>install the seat</u>.

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *The volume control <u>can be adjusted</u>.*
- [STE] **STE:** <u>You can adjust</u> the volume control.

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *The temperature <u>must be adjusted</u>.*
- [STE] **STE:** <u>Adjust</u> the temperature.

- [Non-STE] **Non-STE:** *The sleeve <u>will be adjusted</u> by the robot.*
- [STE] **STE:** The robot <u>will adjust</u> the sleeve.
