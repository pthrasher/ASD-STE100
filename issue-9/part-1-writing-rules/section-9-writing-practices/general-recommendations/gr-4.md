# GR-4 The pronoun "this"

> **GR-4** **The pronoun “this”**

When you use the pronoun “this” in a sentence, make sure that the reader knows the item the pronoun refers to. If “this” can refer to more than one item, give the applicable context again.

**Examples:**

- [Neutral] **Do not write:** *Make sure that the cover is not locked (<u>this</u> can cause damage to the probe).*

  (Which is the cause of damage to the probe? The cover in the locked condition? Or the cover in the unlocked condition?)
- [STE] **WRITE:** Make sure that the cover is not locked. If the cover is locked, <u>this</u> can cause damage to the probe.
- Or
- [STE] If the cover is locked, damage to the probe can occur.

- [Neutral] **Do not write:** *Do not use crocus cloth on aluminum parts. If you do <u>this</u>, you can cause corrosion on aluminum parts. Crocus cloth contains ferrous oxide.*
- [STE] **WRITE:** Do not use crocus cloth on aluminum parts. Crocus cloth contains ferrous oxide, which can cause corrosion on aluminum parts.
