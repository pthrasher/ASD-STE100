# Rule 6.2

> **Rule 6.2** **Use key words and key phrases to give your text a logical structure.**

<u>Key words</u> are words that occur in a text to connect different ideas, and <u>key phrases</u> are phrases that have the same function.

These key words and key phrases show how information in a text is related and give the text a logical structure.

You can also use <u>connecting words</u> and <u>connecting phrases</u> to help the reader understand the logical flow of ideas in the text. Connecting words and connecting phrases have the function of traffic signs, which tell the reader if the information is new, different, or a result of previous information. Examples of approved connecting words are: “and,” “but,” “then,” “thus,” and examples of connecting phrases are “as a result,” and “at the same time.”

The example that follows is the STE text of the example for rule [6.1](rule-6-01.md). In the text, you can see how the underlined key words and key phrases connect sentences and their related ideas. This makes the text much easier to read and understand.

When you use key words and key phrases, make sure that you do not change them in your text. The same terminology will keep your text clear and correct.

**Example in STE:**

| <u>Sentence 1</u> | <u>Sentence 2</u> |
|---|---|
| The Instrument Landing <u>System</u> (the system) on the aircraft <u>shows</u> data that helps the <u>pilot</u> during the approach to the runway. | This <u>system</u> <u>shows </u>the <u>pilot</u> the deviations from the localizer course and the glideslope path. |

Sentence 2 uses the key words “system,” “show,” and “pilot” again to add more information about sentence 1.

| <u>Sentence 2</u> | <u>Sentences 3 and 4</u> |
|---|---|
| This system shows the pilot the deviations from the <u>localizer course</u> and the <u>glideslope path</u>. | The <u>localizer course</u> aligns with the centerline of the runway. And the <u>glideslope path</u> is at a constant angle to the threshold of the runway. |

Sentences 3 and 4 use the key words “localizer course” and “glideslope path” again and add new information.

| <u>Sentence 3</u> | <u>Sentence 4</u> |
|---|---|
| The localizer course aligns with the centerline of the <u>runway</u>. | And the glideslope path is at a constant angle to the threshold of the <u>runway</u>. |

Sentences 3 and 4 also include the key word “runway,” which refers back to sentence 1.

| <u>Sentences 3 and 4</u> | <u>Sentence 5</u> |
|---|---|
| The <u>localizer</u> course aligns with the centerline of the <u>runway</u>. And the <u>glideslope</u> path is at a constant angle to the threshold of the <u>runway</u>. | During the approach to the <u>runway</u>, deviation pointers in the course indicators show the pilot in which direction the aircraft must go:<br>- Left or right (for the <u>localizer</u>)<br>- Up or down (for the <u>glideslope)</u>. |

Sentence 5 also connects to the previous sentences 3 and 4 with the key word “runway” again. Then, sentence 5 gives more information about the function of indicators for the localizer and the glideslope.

| <u>Sentence 2</u> | <u>Sentence 5</u> |
|---|---|
| This system <u>shows the pilot</u> the <u>deviations</u> from the <u>localizer</u> course and the <u>glideslope</u> path. | During the approach to the runway, <u>deviation</u> pointers in the course indicators <u>show the pilot</u> in which direction the aircraft must go:<br>- Left or right (for the <u>localizer</u>)<br>- Up or down (for the <u>glideslope</u>). |

Key words and key phrases (for example, “show(s) the pilot”) give a logical connection between sentences 2 and 5.

<!-- page 98 | 1-6-4 -->

| <u>Sentence 1</u> | <u>Sentence 5</u> |
|---|---|
| The Instrument Landing System in the aircraft <u>shows</u> data that helps the <u>pilot</u> <u>during the approach to the runway</u>. | <u>During the approach to the runway,</u> deviation pointers in the course indicators <u>show the pilot</u> in which direction the aircraft must go:<br>- Left or right (for the localizer)<br>- Up or down (for the glideslope). |

Sentences 1 and 5 are also connected to make a logical paragraph.

Sentence 5 also uses the key phrase “during the approach to the runway” from sentence 1 again to refer to the initial sentence and give more information about it. When you use the same key words and key phrases again, the sentences have a logical connection. If you use different terminology, your text will not be easy to read.

There is also a logical connection between the two paragraphs.

<u>Paragraph 1</u> (Sentences 1 thru 5): <u>deviation</u>, <u>data</u>, <u>localizer</u>, <u>glideslope</u>

<u>Paragraph 2</u> (Sentences 6 and 7): This <u>data</u> about <u>deviations</u> from the <u>localizer course and glideslope path</u> comes from two VHF transceivers. These transceivers transmit this <u>data</u> to Air Traffic Control.
