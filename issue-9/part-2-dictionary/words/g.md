# G

<!-- page 259 | 2-1-G1 -->
## gain (n)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: INCREASE (v)
  - STE: AFTER THE LAST MODIFICATION, THE SYSTEM’S EFFICIENCY INCREASED.
  - Non-STE: The system’s efficiency showed a gain after the last modification.

## gain (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: GET (v)
  - STE: GET ACCESS TO THE CONNECTORS AT THE REAR OF THE INSTRUMENT.
  - Non-STE: Gain access to the connectors at the back of the instrument.

## gall (v)
- Status: not approved
- Alternative: GALLING (TN)

## GET (v)
- Status: approved
- Forms: GETS, GOT
- Help (word column): No other verb forms. <!-- sic: final period printed in teal -->
- Meaning: To obtain, to come into the state of having
  - STE: IF YOU GET THE FLUID ON YOUR SKIN, FLUSH YOUR SKIN IMMEDIATELY WITH CLEAN WATER.
  - Help: Do not use this word to mean BECOME, GO, DECREASE, INCREASE. Refer to rule [9.2](../../part-1-writing-rules/section-9-writing-practices/rule-9-02.md) and the entries from “get” until “get to” that follow.
